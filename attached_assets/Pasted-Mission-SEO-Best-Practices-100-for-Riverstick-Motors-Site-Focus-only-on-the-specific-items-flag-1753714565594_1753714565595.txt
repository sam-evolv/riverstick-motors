## 🟨 SEO FIXES (currently 58)

### 1. Page is blocked from indexing
- Check for any of the following and remove if found:
  - `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`
  - `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` in HTTP headers
  - Rules in `robots.txt` blocking access to `/`
- Ensure the homepage and all core pages are indexable by default.

### 2. Links are not crawlable
- Inspect all `<a>` elements.
  - Ensure they contain valid `href` attributes (not `javascript:void(0)` or missing).
  - Avoid buttons or divs masquerading as links.
- Example fix: Change `<a onclick="...">` to proper `<a href="..." role="button">`.
