You are a senior front-end quality engineer. Your task is to audit and fully optimise the Riverstick Motors website for visual consistency, responsiveness, and full functional behaviour across all devices — including iPhones, Androids, iPads (portrait and landscape), and large desktop screens.

Do not change any branding, structure, or content intention. Focus only on ensuring the website looks just as beautiful and functions just as smoothly on smaller screens as it does on a full desktop display.

Apply the following actions:

---

✅ 1. RESPONSIVE LAYOUT & MEDIA QUERIES

- Review all sections, containers, text blocks, buttons, and images to ensure they scale fluidly using responsive units (`%`, `vw`, `vh`, `em`, or `rem`).
- Eliminate any fixed pixel widths or heights that cause overflow or squashing on mobile screens.
- Ensure margins and padding scale with screen size to maintain elegant spacing and alignment.
- For text-heavy sections, adjust line spacing and font size using `clamp()` or media queries.
- On mobile, reduce any oversized buttons or headlines that crowd the viewport.

---

✅ 2. NAVIGATION FIXES & Z-INDEX LAYERS

- Ensure the dropdown mobile menu (hamburger menu) is **always clickable**, even when the page is fully scrolled to the top.
- Assign it a high `z-index` (e.g. `9999`) and `position: fixed` or `absolute` to ensure it's layered above all hero or header content.
- Ensure no hero video or banner image overlaps or blocks clicks — use `pointer-events: none` or lower `z-index` where necessary.
- Maintain full visibility of navigation links on all devices.

---

✅ 3. HERO SECTION RESPONSIVENESS

- Scale any background images or hero videos appropriately to avoid cropping or overflow.
- Ensure videos have fallback `poster` images and are no larger than 3MB (prefer `.webm` format).
- Avoid text overlapping with the background on smaller screens — use media queries to adjust positioning or layout.

---

✅ 4. IMAGE & ASSET OPTIMISATION

- Compress all `.jpg`, `.png`, and `.webp` images using lossy or lossless methods (80% compression preferred).
- Ensure `img` tags include responsive styling: `width: 100%`, `height: auto`, and avoid setting fixed pixel dimensions.
- Use `object-fit: cover` where needed to maintain visual consistency.

---

✅ 5. BUTTONS & TAP TARGETS

- Ensure all buttons are:
  - Easily tappable on mobile (minimum 44px height)
  - Well-spaced apart to avoid accidental taps
  - Visually strong with hover and active states
- Confirm all CTA buttons are properly linked or gracefully disabled if not ready (e.g. RSA "Book Now").

---

✅ 6. INTERACTION & ACCESSIBILITY

- Ensure all links and buttons are keyboard-navigable.
- Add visible `:focus` states to interactive elements.
- Confirm all images have meaningful `alt` attributes.
- Validate proper tab order from nav → main content → footer.

---

✅ 7. MOBILE MENU PERFORMANCE

- Ensure mobile nav opens and closes smoothly with appropriate animations or transitions.
- Prevent content shift or scroll-lock when menu is opened.
- Test tap area and visibility on devices under 400px wide.

---

✅ 8. SECTION SPACING & SCROLLING EXPERIENCE

- Audit vertical spacing between sections on small screens to avoid crowded or cluttered appearance.
- Use consistent scroll spacing for headers, CTA sections, and footers.
- Ensure no horizontal scrolling is possible on mobile (i.e., remove all unintended overflow).

---

✅ 9. VISUAL BALANCE & TYPOGRAPHY

- Use consistent font sizing, hierarchy, and colour contrast across devices.
- Ensure headings do not dominate or break layout on mobile.
- Smooth out any abrupt line breaks, text overflow, or inconsistent alignments.

---

✅ 10. FINAL MOBILE + TABLET QA

- Simulate viewports for:
  - iPhone 13–16 (Safari)
  - Samsung Galaxy (Chrome)
  - iPad (portrait + landscape)
  - MacBook + large desktop (1080p+)
- Confirm all sections render cleanly, all content is visible, and no visual bugs or interaction issues occur.

---

Ensure no core design elements are lost or degraded. Maintain the premium, elegant visual style across all screen sizes. Do not use default mobile simplifications — this site must feel high-end on both desktop and mobile.

Only output the optimised responsive code. Prepare it for deployment immediately after testing.
