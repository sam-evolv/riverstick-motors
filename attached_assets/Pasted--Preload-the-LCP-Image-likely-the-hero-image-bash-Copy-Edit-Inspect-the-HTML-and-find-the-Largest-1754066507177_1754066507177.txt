Inspect the HTML and find the Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) image — likely the hero or top banner image. Add this preload tag into the <head> section:

<link rel="preload" as="image" href="/path-to-hero.webp" type="image/webp">

This improves first meaningful paint without altering layout.

Edit

Do not alter layout, carousel timing, or responsive behaviour.
